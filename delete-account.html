Delete Your MeCanIQ Account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the appOpen MeCanIQ, then go to Settings, Your Data, Delete Account, and confirm. This also erases the data on your phone. Without the appEmail mecaniq.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include your username. Send it from the email address on your account if it has one. We'll reply when it's done. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. Cancel your subscription tooDeleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings, or you may keep being charged. What gets deletedYour username, any email address and password, and all your vehicles, service history and other records on our servers. What's keptPurchase records stay with Apple or Google and RevenueCat. Crash reports, which aren't linked to you, expire on their own. Copies in routine server backups are removed as those backups expire. No account?If you never set up an account, your records aren't on our servers. Deleting the app removes them.
