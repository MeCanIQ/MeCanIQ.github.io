Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

MeCanIQ ("the app," "we," "us") helps you track vehicle maintenance, costs, and important dates. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. What we collectAccount information: if you set up an account, the username you choose - and, only if you decide to add them, an email address and password, used to back up your garage and sign in on another device. You can use the app without an account. Vehicle and maintenance data you enter: make, model, trim, year, mileage, state, driving habits, service/maintenance history, costs you enter, insurance policy details, registration/inspection/license expiration dates, and accident or incident log entries. It stays on your phone unless you set up an account, which backs it up so you can sign in on another device. Photos you attach to accident or incident entries stay on your phone and are not uploaded. Subscription status: whether you're subscribed and to which plan. The app never sees or stores your payment card details - purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat (a subscription-management service) helps us know which features you've unlocked. Device-local preferences: notification settings and display theme, kept on your device. Crash and error reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report so we can fix it - what went wrong, where in the app's code, the app version, your device model and operating system version, and a random ID for this install of the app. These reports are not linked to your name, email, username, or vehicles. We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing history, or any data for advertising purposes, and we do not run ad or tracking SDKs of any kind. How we use itSolely to run the app's own features: estimating part wear and health, sending you reminders, tracking expiration dates, and calculating the costs and savings figures you see. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, each of which only processes it on our behalf to make the app work. Who we share it withSupabase - hosts our database and handles account sign-in. Your account and vehicle data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. RevenueCat - manages subscription status once you purchase a plan. It receives your purchase receipt from Apple/Google to confirm your entitlement, not your payment details. Apple App Store / Google Play - process the actual subscription payment. We never see your card number. Sentry - receives the crash and error reports described above, so we can find and fix problems. It is not used for advertising or tracking. NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) - when the app checks for open recalls, it sends your vehicle's make/model/year to NHTSA's public recall database. This is a public government lookup, not a submission of any personal or account information. Your choicesExport your data anytime from Settings > Export My Data. Delete your account anytime from Settings > Your Data > Delete Account - this permanently removes your account and all vehicle data from our systems. This can't be undone. You can also request deletion without the app at https://mecaniq.github.io/delete-account.html. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription - cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Purchase records stay with Apple or Google and RevenueCat. Crash reports, which aren't linked to you, expire on their own, and copies in routine server backups are removed as those backups expire. Contact us with any privacy question at mecaniq.app@gmail.com. Children's privacyMeCanIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Changes to this policyIf this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
