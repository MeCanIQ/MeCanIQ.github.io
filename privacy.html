Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

MeCanIQ ("the app," "we," "us") helps you track vehicle maintenance, costs, and important dates. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. What we collect Account information: if you set up an account, the username you choose - and, only if you decide to add them, an email address and password, used to back up your garage and sign in on another device. You can use the app without an account. Passwords: your password is never stored in a form anyone can read - not by us, not by our providers, and not in the app's code or its crash reports. Only a scrambled, one-way version of it (a salted hash) is kept, which can check a password you type but can't be turned back into it. The app doesn't save your password on your phone (your phone's own password manager may offer to, if you use one), and it only ever sends it, encrypted, to our sign-in service when you set it or sign in. Repeated wrong guesses are slowed down or locked out for a while. Even so, please use a password you don't use anywhere else. Vehicle and maintenance data you enter: make, model, trim, year, mileage, state, driving habits, service/maintenance history, costs you enter, insurance policy details, registration/inspection/license expiration dates, and accident or incident log entries. It stays on your phone unless you set up an account, which backs it up so you can sign in on another device. Photos you add, of an accident or of your documents (like your license or insurance card), are kept on your phone and are never uploaded to us. A photo you take with the app's camera is also saved to your phone's photo library, so if your library syncs to a service like iCloud Photos or Google Photos, that copy syncs too. Like the rest of the app's data on your phone, the app's records and photos can be included in your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup). Subscription status: whether you're subscribed and to which plan. The app never sees or stores your payment card details - purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat (a subscription-management service) helps us know which features you've unlocked. Device-local preferences: notification settings and display theme, kept on your device. Waitlist: if you join the launch waitlist on our website, your email address and, if you tell us, whether you use an iPhone or Android. It's used only to email you when the app is available, it isn't linked to any account, and we'll remove it whenever you ask at mecaniq.app@gmail.com. Crash and error reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report so we can fix it - what went wrong, where in the app's code, the app version, your device model and operating system version, and a random ID for this install of the app. These reports are not linked to your name, email, username, or vehicles. We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing history, or any data for advertising purposes, and we do not run ad or tracking SDKs of any kind. Do Not Track: we don't track you across other apps or websites, so the app and our website don't treat Do Not Track signals any differently - there's nothing to switch off. How we use it Solely to run the app's own features: estimating part wear and health, sending you reminders, tracking expiration dates, and calculating the costs and savings figures you see - and, if you joined the waitlist, emailing you when the app is available. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, each of which only processes it on our behalf to make the app work. Who we share it with Supabase - hosts our database and handles account sign-in. Your account and vehicle data, and the waitlist, are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. RevenueCat - manages subscription status once you purchase a plan. It receives your purchase receipt from Apple/Google to confirm your entitlement, not your payment details. Apple App Store / Google Play - process the actual subscription payment. We never see your card number. Sentry - receives the crash and error reports described above, so we can find and fix problems. It is not used for advertising or tracking. NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) - when the app checks for open recalls, it sends your vehicle's make/model/year to NHTSA's public recall database. This is a public government lookup, not a submission of any personal or account information. Expo - delivers updates to the app. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives the app's version, your phone's operating system and a random ID for this install of the app - not your name, account or vehicles. Like any internet service, each of these providers sees your device's IP address when the app or our website connects to it. We don't add it to your account or use it to identify you. Links to other sites Some buttons open another website in your browser - for example a parts store (like Amazon or AutoZone), a how-to video search on YouTube, a map search for a dealer, or nhtsa.gov. These links include a search for your vehicle and the part or place, so that site receives that search. What happens there is covered by that site's own privacy policy, not this one. Your choices Export your data anytime from Settings > Your Data > Export My Data. Delete your account anytime from Settings > Your Data > Delete Account - this permanently removes your account and all vehicle data from our systems, and erases it from this phone, including the app's own copies of your photos. Copies the app's camera saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them yourself. This can't be undone. You can also request deletion without the app at https://mecaniq.github.io/delete-account.html. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription - cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Purchase records stay with Apple or Google and RevenueCat. Crash reports, which aren't linked to you, expire on their own, and copies in routine server backups are removed as those backups expire. Contact us with any privacy question at mecaniq.app@gmail.com. Children's privacy MeCanIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
